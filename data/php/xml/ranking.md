# PHP × XML: selected parsers (pilot)

10 control-verified third-party paths selected; the cell is at the ten-path ceiling, and pear/xml_parser qualified but ranks eleventh ([displaced](ceiling-displaced.json)). PROTOCOL.md lets a wrapper qualify as a package API with its parser family identified, so libxml2-family wrappers are not excluded for that reason alone. Remaining lower-ranked wrappers stay unresolved until qualified and tested; prewk/xml-string-streamer and jtrumbull/xml-parser were excluded by valid-control probes, and laravie/parser and orchestra/parser as mapping layers over SimpleXML.

The four DOM loaders verified on 2026-11-18 (QueryPath, laminas-xml, xml-common, DiDOM) come from classifying every snapshot hit with at least pear/xml_parser's downloads that had no lead row ([classification](registry-snapshot.json)). Each documented entry point hands back a namespace-aware DOM built by libxml2, so both controls are compared with the full namespace-aware expat summary. Their own PHP lines are identical on both controls, so, like Parsel and pear/xml_parser, they are labelled wrapper paths: all 360-362 input-dependent AFL++ tuples of their native overlays arise while libxml2 parses, checked against a bare loader with each package's options (DiDOM's includes its trim and `preserveWhiteSpace = false`).
